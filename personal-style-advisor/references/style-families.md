# Style families worldwide

Written 2026-11. A working map of style families, written fresh for this skill. Families mix:
most people are a blend of two or three. Use the person's own words and culture first.

## How to choose families for a person

1. Personality: the three words for how they want to come across (e.g. "calm, capable, warm").
2. Lifestyle and dress codes (office, campus, creative, outdoor, religious, family events).
3. Colouring: clear colouring carries crisp, graphic styles; muted colouring suits tonal, textured
   styles; deep colouring carries rich, dark styles; light colouring suits airy, lighter styles.
4. Frame and features: fine frames suit lighter details; broad frames carry strong structure and
   bold prints; angular features echo sharp tailoring; soft features echo drape and curves.
5. Culture: include their tradition's styles as full families, not as "ethnic accents".

When someone is unsure, describe three to six families that fit, with one look each for their
occasion, and let them choose.

## Global families

| Family | Mood | Signature pieces | Who it often suits | Occasions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic / timeless | calm, trustworthy | tailored blazer, straight trousers, white shirt, trench, loafers, pearl or gold studs | balanced proportions, medium contrast, people who want reliability | office, interviews, formal day events |
| Minimalist | quiet, precise | clean lines, monochrome, no logos, quality fabric, structured bags | clear or medium contrast; people who dislike fuss | city work, creative offices |
| Quiet luxury / "old money" | understated, polished | knitwear, camel coat, fine leather, tonal neutrals, small gold | any; relies on fit and fabric over brand | client meetings, travel, dinners |
| Relaxed / natural | easy, approachable | linen, cotton, relaxed trousers, overshirts, sandals, wooden or matte jewellery | broader frames, muted colouring, outdoor lifestyles | weekends, warm climates, casual offices |
| Romantic / soft | gentle, warm | ruffles, florals, soft drape, bows, pastels or warm brights, wrap dresses | soft features, curves, light or soft colouring (anyone who wants it) | dates, garden parties, festivals |
| Dramatic / bold | striking, confident | sharp shoulders, strong colour blocks, statement jewellery, long lines | tall or angular, high contrast (but anyone can borrow) | galas, evening, stage |
| Gamine / playful | lively, youthful | cropped pieces, stripes, loafers, short hair, mixing | petite, compact frames, animated features | creative work, casual |
| Streetwear | current, social | sneakers, hoodies, graphic tees, cargo, caps, layering | anyone; strong in youth and creative scenes | casual, campus, music events |
| Smart casual / "business casual" | relaxed professional | chinos, knit polos, loafers, unstructured blazers, kurta with trousers | most | office Fridays, start-ups |
| Sporty / athleisure | active, practical | performance knits, joggers, trainers, technical outerwear | active lifestyles | gym, travel, errands |
| Bohemian | free, artistic | prints, fringe, layered jewellery, maxi skirts, block prints, embroidery | muted or warm colouring, relaxed frames | festivals, travel, creative events |
| Edgy / rock | rebellious | leather, black, boots, studs, distressed denim | high contrast, angular | concerts, nightlife |
| Preppy | neat, collegiate | button-downs, cable knits, blazers, pleated skirts, loafers | clear colouring | campus, casual office |
| Avant-garde / conceptual | intellectual, experimental | asymmetry, deconstruction, volume, black | confident wearers | art, fashion events |
| Androgynous / gender-neutral | balanced, modern | relaxed tailoring, overshirts, straight trousers, unisex knits, boots | anyone; strong in creative workplaces | everyday, creative work |
| Modest fashion | covered, graceful | maxi skirts, long tunics, wide trousers, layering, abayas, hijabs | anyone dressing modestly; can be minimalist, romantic or bold | all |
| Utility / workwear | practical, grounded | chore jackets, cargo pockets, canvas, boots | broader frames, muted colouring | casual, outdoor |
| Vintage / retro | nostalgic | era pieces (1950s swing, 1970s flares, 1990s slip dresses) | depends on era | themed events, creative |

## Regional and traditional families (full families, not accents)

| Family | Mood | Signature pieces | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Indian festive / wedding glam | celebratory, rich | lehengas, Banarasi and Kanjivaram sarees, sherwanis, bandhgalas, kundan and polki jewellery | colour and weight rules in regional-wear.md |

## Mixing families: a quick recipe

Pick a **base** family for silhouettes (e.g. Classic), a **second** for colour or texture (e.g.
Indian handloom), and an **accent** for one item (e.g. Streetwear sneakers). Example: an ivory
handloom kurta (handloom) cut straight with a band collar (classic) worn with straight trousers and
clean white sneakers (street) for a creative office.
