# Shopping by country

**Verify before quoting. Compiled 2026-11 from general knowledge of well-known retailers; store
names, ranges, ownership and availability change, and some may have closed or left a market.**
Use web search for live details and prices. **No prices are listed on purpose**: never invent them.
Budget tiers here are described by store type, and the person's own budget sets the tier.

Use this file only after the person asks for shopping help (Step 8).

## How to give shopping help

1. **Re-wear and re-style first**: what they own, then borrowing, renting, tailoring or
   second-hand, then new.
2. Name **store types and two or three examples per tier** for their country. Give search terms
   ("silk-cotton saree mustard maroon border"), not just store names.
3. Sizing: remind them sizes vary by brand; check the brand's size chart in cm; tailoring fixes most fit.
4. Budget: convert nothing yourself without a live rate; if web search is available, check current
   prices in the local currency and say the date.
5. Never push one retailer. No affiliate links.
6. **Where to shop**: when home and event countries differ, buy and alter at home (fittings need
   time), and use the event city only for small extras. Within the EU, cross-border online orders
   have no customs duty and come with EU return rights; outside it, check import duties and returns.
7. **Budget split without prices**: suggest shares, not numbers (for example most of it on the main
   garment, a smaller share for tailoring, and the rest for accessories and shoes), then look up live
   prices if web search is available.

Tiers used below: **Budget** (mass market, markets, marketplaces), **Mid** (national chains,
mid-price brands), **Premium** (designer, luxury, couture). **Tailor / made-to-measure**,
**Rent**, **Second-hand** are listed separately because they cut cost and waste.

Regional sources (India):
- **Kerala / Kochi**: large textile stores on MG Road and Broadway (for example Seematti,
  Jayalakshmi, Kalyan Silks), Hantex and Kerala state handloom outlets for kasavu, ready-to-wear
  set-mundu and kasavu sets online.
- **Weaving towns** (buy from co-operatives or known weavers): Kanchipuram and Arani (Tamil Nadu silk),
  Varanasi (Banarasi), Paithan and Yeola (Paithani), Pochampally (ikat), Chanderi and Maheshwar (MP),
  Bhagalpur (tussar), Sualkuchi (Assam silk).
- **Silk authenticity**: look for the Silk Mark label (India's Silk Mark Organisation) and, for zari,
  ask whether it is pure or tested zari; burn tests on a loose thread are a traditional check.
- **Menswear ethnic**: city wedding-wear streets and Manyavar-type chains; veshtis from Ramraj and
  co-op stores; men's juttis and mojaris from the same markets and online.

Size notes: Indian ethnic wear is often sold as unstitched fabric (blouse piece, suit set) to be
tailored; ready-made kurtas use S-XXL or chest sizes; sarees are free size (check length: 5.5 m
standard, 6.3 m with blouse piece, 9 yards for Nauvari and Madisar).

## Size conversion notes

- Always use the brand's own chart in centimetres. International size labels (S/M/L, UK 10, US 6,
  EU 38) don't line up across brands.
- Asian brands often run smaller than European and US ones; check measurements, not letters.
- Petite, tall and plus ranges vary a lot by country; tailoring is often cheaper and better in South
  and Southeast Asia, West Africa and the Middle East.
- Shoes: measure foot length in cm and use the brand chart.
- Seated users: compare garment measurements to seated measurements (see body-proportions.md).
