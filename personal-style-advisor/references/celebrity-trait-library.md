# Celebrity trait library (seed list)

Compiled 2026-11. **This is a seed list, not a closed list.** Extend it with live web search
(prefer fashion press: Vogue and its editions, Elle, Harper's Bazaar, GQ, Business of Fashion,
Style Rave, BellaNaija, Femina, Filmfare, regional equivalents) and check that recent looks still
fit the recipe.

## Rules

- Match on **traits**: build and frame, height range, colouring family (depth, contrast, warm or
  cool look), face-framing family, style personality, culture and profession. **Never** match on
  facial resemblance, and never tell a person they look like someone.
- Heights are **reported** figures that vary by source, so they are given as ranges (petite / average /
  tall). Colouring is what public photos show and is affected by lighting, makeup and editing (Low
  confidence). Face families are Low confidence: published guides often disagree on celebrity face
  shapes.
- Styling in public images is the work of stylists, so the recipe copies **repeatable moves**, not
  exact outfits or price levels.
- Respect identities: use the pronouns the person has stated publicly (check, they can change).
  Disability, religion and gender identity appear only where the person speaks about them publicly
  and it's relevant to the clothing.
- Confidence for the overall match: say how many traits are shared. Two or three shared traits is
  typical and fine.

Columns: **Build** (frame and proportions as publicly visible), **Height** (reported range),
**Colouring** (as seen in public photos), **Face family** (Low confidence), **Style personality**,
**Recipe** (repeatable moves), **Conf.** (confidence in the trait description: M = medium, L = low).

## How to turn a match into a recipe for the user

1. List shared traits (e.g. "deep warm colouring, petite, loves heritage sarees").
2. Pick three moves from the figure's recipe that fit those traits.
3. Adapt each to the user's culture, climate, modesty, budget and occasion, e.g. "Vidya Balan's
   handloom-and-contrast-border move → a silk-cotton Kanjivaram in mustard with a maroon border for
   a Tamil wedding in Chennai heat; elbow sleeves; a lighter temple necklace if you're petite."
4. Say what not to copy (e.g. a look built for a tall frame, or a price level).
