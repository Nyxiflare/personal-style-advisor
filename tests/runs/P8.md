### For your occasion

**Look 1: a ready-stitched kasavu saree with a peacock green blouse (my main suggestion)**
- **Saree:** off-white kasavu in soft, fine cotton with a gold border, made up as a ready-stitched
  saree. Off-white is warm ivory (#F0E2C8) and the gold border is a warm saffron-gold (#C88A1C).
  - The pleats are sewn flat into a soft, wide elastic waistband. It has one side opening that

  - Ask for the front to be cut a little longer than the back, so it sits evenly when you sit.
  - The hem should reach your ankle, not the floor, so it clears the footrests.
  - No thick seams, knots or tucks at the back where you sit.
- **Pallu:** pleated and stitched in place, then fixed to the blouse at the left shoulder with a
  large press stud or a closed pin placed at the back. Keep it short enough to end at your lap, well
  away from the wheels.

  border rest on the blouse, not on your skin. Before buying, rub the border on the inside of your
  wrist. Some kasavu borders are much softer than others.
- **Blouse:** peacock green, a deep blue-green (#1D5C58), in soft cotton lined with mulmul.

- **Footwear:** soft slip-ons with a closed back, or velcro sandals, that stay on the footrests.

## Skill defects

1. **Too many rounds before help for an older or tired user (SKILL.md Steps 1, 2 and 4).** Step 1
   (four compound questions plus words/photos), Step 2 (traits) and the mandatory Step 4 check-in mean
   three full question rounds before any advice. Each Step 1 item is really 3-5 sub-questions; item 3
   alone asks about date, venue, time, dress code and outfit type. For a 72-year-old this is heavy.
   *Change:* add a "light path" rule. When the person is words-only and the occasion is clear, merge
   the Step 2 colour questions with the Step 4 check-in. Cap each round at about four short
   questions, and say "one question per line, no sub-clauses" for older users or anyone who says
   things are a struggle.

2. **The Step 2 trait table has no "ask only what changes the advice" filter (SKILL.md Step 2).** It
   lists height, shoulder vs hip, torso vs leg, face widths, 3+3 personality words, life and practical
   items. For a full-time seated user, height and torso-leg balance barely matter, and face-shape
   questions are tiring. I dropped them on my own judgement, and that left face framing unread
   (Low). *Change:* add "For seated users skip height and torso-leg; for event-only requests skip
   'Life'; ask face questions only if necklines or glasses are in play."

3. **Grip, arthritis and carer-assisted dressing are barely covered (body-proportions.md section
   11).** One-handed closures appear only under "Prosthetics and limb differences", and carer
   dressing is one line ("dress the less mobile side first"). Nothing covers limited grip from
   arthritis (magnetic buttons, loop pulls, no small hooks on blouses), dressing while seated in the
   chair, heat and sweat when seated, pressure points from saree tucks and knots at the back, or
   toilet access during a long event. *Change:* add an "Arthritis / limited grip" row and a "Seated
   saree" checklist: waistband type, front-longer cut, ankle hem to clear footrests, pallu length
   ending at the lap, pin placement.

4. **No guidance on scratchy embellishment in traditional wear (regional-wear.md A2 Kasavu row and
   A9).** Kasavu is listed only as "off-white cotton with gold border". The zari and sensitive-skin
   problem, which is very common in older wearers, appears only as "line blouse embroidery with soft
   cotton" in body-proportions.md. I had to improvise: lining the waistband, keeping the border on the
   blouse side, and the inner-wrist feel test. *Change:* add a "Sensitive skin" note to A2/A9
   (softer border options, lining, feel test). Don't claim specific "soft zari" products unless
   sourced.

5. **Kerala is thin across the references.** regional-wear.md has Mundum Neriyathum and Kasavu rows
