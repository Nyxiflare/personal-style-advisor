### General rules for you
- **Near your face:** teal, aubergine, warm ivory or off-white, deep soft brown, deep olive or forest greens. **Keep away from the face:** pinks (they grey you), and stark optic white with black right at the face. Black is fine in the gown itself, since the hijab sits between it and your face.

- **Fit:** loose everywhere, shoulder seams at your shoulder bone, sleeves long enough to reach your wrist bone when your arm is raised to take the scroll.

### Cap over hijab: practical steps
1. Wrap the hijab **flat on the crown**: no volumiser, no high bun on top, so the cap sits level.
2. Use a **non-slip underscarf** (cotton or jersey) and a **matte** hijab fabric. Silky satin lets the cap slide.
3. Put the cap on **last**. Secure it with 2-4 pins through the cap's inner band into the underscarf (not the outer hijab, which would pull the drape).
4. Practise at home once with the cap, or a similar flat hat, and take photos from the front and side.
5. Bring spare pins and a small mirror.
- **Hijab drape** (face framing isn't read yet, so pick by feel): if you'd like your face to look a little longer, wrap closer, meeting just below the chin with a slight point. For a softer, wider frame, drape with gentle volume at the sides.

### Budget (AED 800): where to put it
