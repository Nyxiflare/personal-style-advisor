# Persona tests

Eight fictional people. Each has an **opening message** (all the stylist sees at turn 1), a
**hidden sheet** (what the simulated user reveals only when asked), and for some a **photo
description** standing in for an uploaded photo (the test runner can't attach real photos).

## Rubric (applied to every transcript)

| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Asked country and budget first | turn 1 asks for country/city and budget (unless already given) before any analysis |
| C2 | Asked what they want to wear and where | turn 1 asks occasion, setting and the outfit type they have in mind |
| C3 | Confidence levels | every reading (undertone, value, chroma, contrast, face, proportions) carries High/Medium/Low with a reason |
| C4 | No body-shaming or colourist language | none of: hide, flaws, slimming, problem areas, brighten/lighten your skin, fair = better, "too dark", "despite your size" |
| C5 | Celebrities by trait with a recipe | 2-4 public figures, traits named, a style recipe adapted to the user; no face lookalike claims |
| C6 | Shopping last | no store names or prices before the user asks; shopping offered as a last option |
| C7 | Modesty and culture respected | every look meets the stated modesty, religion, venue and family customs |
| C8 | Check-in before advising | readings shown and confirmed (or marked unconfirmed) before outfit advice |

## P1. Meenakshi, Chennai wedding (words only)

**Opening:** "Hi! My cousin's wedding is next month and I have no idea what to wear. I'm quite
short and pretty dark, and I always feel like I disappear in photos."

**Hidden sheet:** womenswear; India, Chennai; wedding in Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu, morning muhurtham plus evening
reception; budget around ₹15,000 for the saree, will reuse gold jewellery from her mother; 152 cm;
deep skin with a golden-warm look, very dark brown eyes, black hair; compliments in mustard,
maroon and emerald; peach and baby pink make her look "ashy"; gold looks better than silver;
foundation shades often turn grey on her; hips a little wider than shoulders; wants to come across
"elegant, confident, traditional"; Hindu Tamil family, sarees expected for the muhurtham; heat
and humidity matter.
